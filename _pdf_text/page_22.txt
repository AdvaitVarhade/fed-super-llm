Several directions remain for future research. Incorpo-
rating temporal dynamics and drift-aware updates may
further improve adaptability in rapidly evolving platforms,

performance under sparse interaction regimes. Additionally,
extending the framework to privacy-preserving or federated
environments represents an important step toward real-world
deployment.
Overall, SUPER provides a scalable and model-agnostic
pathway toward more balanced and user-aligned recom-
mender systems, supporting the broader objective of deliver-
ing fair, diverse, and behaviorally coherent recommendations.
